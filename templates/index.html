上传要求

仅支持白底红蓝线或白底黑线。CP 务必完整、不缺角，尽量无遮挡并保持接近正方形；程序会自动裁剪外围无关区域。暂时仅支持 22.5° 系设计。White-background red/blue lines or white-background black lines only. The CP must be complete with all four corners visible, as unobstructed as possible, and approximately square; irrelevant surrounding areas are cropped automatically. Currently only 22.5°-system designs are supported.
